Mirroring the Past

Exploring How Ancestral Digital Self Influences History Learning

Gong · Sun · Wang · Hu · Zhong · Zhang · An Shenzhen, China 2026
Trace the timeline
Research premise

Identity as a bridge through time 以身份为媒介，缩短人与历史的心理距离

How does presenting a historical narrator as an “ancestral digital self” influence learners’ experiences and immediate learning outcomes?

Ancestral Digital Self is an AI-generated pedagogical agent in prerecorded videos that mirrors the learner’s facial features and vocal timbre, representing a historically situated version of the self.

本研究将学习者的身份置入距今 3,000 多年的屋背岭文化叙事中，并与非自我教学代理进行对比。

Findings & discussion

Experience and outcome diverge 沉浸体验增强，并不等于短期学习表现更好

A critical trade-off between identity-driven engagement and knowledge acquisition.

+

Closer and more immersed

The Digital Self condition increased psychological closeness, agent perception, narrative transportation, and agent–user relatedness.

±

Familiar, motivating—and uncanny

Self-similarity could encourage exploration and positive self-projection, while also causing discomfort and stronger uncanny responses.

−

No immediate learning advantage

History retention was lower in the Digital Self condition; Remember / Know / Guess judgments showed no reliable differences.
